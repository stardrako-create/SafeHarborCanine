## Latest evidence and remaining work

The full SV catalogue scan covered 141,810 records, including the remote positions of 150 BND ALT entries. It found no overlaps or BND links at w01/2848/0442 and recovered the known w11 deletion record. Breakpoint confidence intervals are unavailable in that source.

The w11 comparison uses a panel of previously reported alternatives, not the complete genome. The same seven junction-spanning read groups have compatible inward geometry on the synthetic junction. Five have finite genomic alternatives with summed alignment-score differences of 25–66; two lack a reported compatible alternative within the tested bounds. Those two have undefined margins, not infinite support. These are repeated analyses of the same reads, not additional independent observations. Full-genome competition, empirical fragment-distribution analysis and structural interpretation remain open.

The wider control pool retains 57 unresolved source ALT entries. These are not 57 problems of w01. Nuclease/variant, cassette and individual dog sequence data are needed for target-specific design. Exact-unique 20 bp segments are not validated guides; PAM, mismatch, bulge and individual-genotype checks remain outstanding.

## Archive contents and reproducibility

This snapshot includes pipeline source, configuration, small result tables, review reports and provenance receipts. Historical version folders are retained; consult the current proposal for the latest interpretation. Historical failed/intermediate receipts do not establish validated results.

Raw sequencing reads, genome/reference binaries, alignment files and indexes, original VCF downloads, large genome-wide tracks, local memory queues, email drafts and teaching materials are outside this snapshot. Existing published genome-wide tracks remain in their Zenodo dataset record; they are not replaced or claimed to be rebuilt by this release. Scripts and provenance record public sources and local input paths. Reproduction requires downloading those inputs, installing dependencies and adapting paths; this archive is not a self-contained end-to-end execution environment.

`PUBLICATION_MANIFEST_20260925.json` records SHA-256 hashes for the selected files at packaging time (excluding itself). Source receipts may also reference local-only intermediate files. No completed whole-pipeline rerun is claimed by this publication.
