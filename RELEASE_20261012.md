## What changed since the 25 September snapshot

- **w11** moved from *conditional* to *not advanced*. The catalogued 28.9-Mb simple deletion was not confirmed by coverage, reference support, combined ROS/UU competition, or the reviewed local assemblies and read recruitment. The partial junction sequence is compatible with a local repetitive alteration, but the complete allele is unresolved.
- **ANO2-39708** was added. It lies in the broad ANO2/NTF3 region (previously listed as reserve w03) and is distinct from w01 and w11. Reference projection, local variant reconciliation, conservation review and full-genome ROS/UU sequence searches were completed; two exploratory 23-nt TTTV profiles had no off-locus matches under the documented substitution/single-bulge/PAM model. These profiles are not approved guides, and actual nuclease specificity has not been established.
- **Inventory review.** The 461 original regional records, the 64 local windows (which occupy only three broad regions) and an independent expansion (3,000 background windows screened, 133 taken to annotation/ATAC triage, 54 in a TAD-clear queue) were reviewed under unchanged criteria. No further candidate received full audit approval; the expansion loci do not satisfy the original convergent 50–75-kb SHIP gap definition and were not promoted.
- The earlier regional rankings and counts, including the historical 26 passing regions, are superseded and should not be read as a list of validated sites.

## Main limits

Sparse direct T-cell accessibility data, incomplete methylation coverage, and incomplete risk-gene annotation (for ANO2, 2,601 of the 2,654 frozen risk symbols were placed in ROS or UU; 53 remain unresolved). Neither w01 nor ANO2-39708 has demonstrated whole-window low methylation, functional isolation, or safe, stable transgene expression. Nuclease- and individual-genotype-dependent specificity checks are outstanding.

## Archive contents and reproducibility

This snapshot contains pipeline source, configuration, small result tables, review reports and provenance receipts through 12 October 2026. Historical version folders are retained for provenance; their candidate rankings are historical.

Not included: raw sequencing reads, genome and annotation downloads, alignments and indexes, large genome-wide tracks, third-party reference data sets (for example assembly annotations and the HGNC gene set), local work queues, e-mail drafts and teaching materials. The genome-wide tracks in the Zenodo dataset record are unchanged from earlier releases; the later rebuilt tracks (v1.20 onward) are not archived. Scripts and receipts record public sources and local input paths; reproduction requires downloading those inputs, installing the dependencies and adapting paths. This archive is not a self-contained end-to-end execution environment, and no whole-pipeline rerun is claimed.

`PUBLICATION_MANIFEST_20261012.json` records SHA-256 hashes of the archived files at packaging time (excluding itself). To check an unpacked archive or a checkout of tag `audit-2026-10-12`: `python scripts/verify_manifest.py PUBLICATION_MANIFEST_20261012.json`.
